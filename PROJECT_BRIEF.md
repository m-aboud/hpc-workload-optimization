# Project Brief

## Objective
Demonstrate production-oriented HPC systems administration and researcher support through transparent, auditable lab artifacts.

## Technical outcomes
- Scheduler efficiency and QOS/accounting review
- Scientific container reproducibility with Apptainer
- MPI/OpenMP/Fortran/OpenACC examples for workload-model support
- Lustre + IOR parallel-I/O performance testing
- OSU-based interconnect latency/bandwidth testing
- Linux node patch/hardening readiness audit
- Researcher-facing troubleshooting playbook

## Business / research outcomes
- Shorter time to diagnose failed or inefficient jobs
- Better utilization of expensive CPU/GPU capacity
- Lower friction for reproducible scientific software environments
- Reduced operational drift and maintenance risk
- Better evidence for capacity and procurement decisions
